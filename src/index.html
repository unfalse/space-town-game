Press F1 to access editor

    Keys in editor:
  • 1 - eraser
  • 2 - indestructible obstacle
  • 3 - bot
  • 4 - destructible obstacle
  • 5 - waypoint
  • 6 - waypoint eraser
  • 7 - player

In editor click on the game field to place objects.

To play the level click "Play" button.

Click "Load" to load saved levels.

In game press "t" to toggle this hint.

Click on the game field to set waypoints for the bot.

Click on existing waypoints to remove them.
